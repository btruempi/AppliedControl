    Fisher Control Valves

    Fisher is the world's leading control valve brand — over 100 years of engineering for every process condition. Applied Control stocks, specifies, and repairs the complete Fisher line.

    Globe Control Valves — The Workhorse Family

    🔵 Fisher ED — General Purpose Globe

    The #1 specified control valve in the world. Sizes 1\" to 12\", flanged or threaded, hundreds of trim options. Equal percentage, linear, and quick-opening characteristics. PTFE or graphite packing. The default valve for liquid, gas, and steam service when nothing exotic is needed.

    Pressure: Up to 740 psi CWP (ANSI 300) | Temp: -100°F to 450°F | Applications: General process control, cooling water, utilities, plant air

    🔵 Fisher EZ — Small Globe, Big Performance

    Compact globe valve for 1/2\" to 2\" service. Same engineering as the ED in a smaller body. Integral actuator option (Fisher 67CFR) reduces installation footprint by 60%. Popular in pharmaceutical plants and tight instrument rack installations.

    Pressure: Up to 1480 psi (ANSI 600) | Temp: -100°F to 450°F | Applications: Sampling systems, injection quills, analyzer sample conditioning

    🔴 Fisher HP — High Pressure Globe

    Built for extreme pressure and temperature — oil & gas wellheads, high-pressure injection, supercritical steam. Forged body construction, stellite trim, and live-loaded graphite packing for zero-leakage in the harshest environments. SIL 2 shutdown service capable.

    Pressure: Up to 10,000 psi | Temp: -320°F to 1000°F | Applications: Gas injection, supercritical steam, HP feed systems, compressor recycle

    ⚪ Fisher GX — Next Generation Globe

    Fisher's newest globe valve platform featuring an integral DVC6200 mount, reduced fugitive emissions, and 50% fewer parts than legacy designs. ENVIRO-SEAL packing as standard. The replacement for aging ED/ET valve populations.

    Pressure: Up to 740 psi CWP | Temp: -100°F to 450°F | Applications: Fugitive emission compliance, LDAR programs, new plant installations

    Rotary Control Valves — Flow Capacity Champions

    🟢 Fisher V150 / V200 / V300 Vee-Ball

    Segmented ball valve with a V-notch ball for characterized flow control. The V150 handles clean liquids, V200 adds trim for general service, and V300 is built for slurries, paper stock, and abrasive fluids with a contoured seat. Dramatically higher Cv than an equivalent globe valve — ideal for high-flow, low-drop applications.

    Cv: Up to 8,000 (12\") | Temp: -40°F to 500°F | Applications: Pulp & paper stock, polymer slurries, mine tailings, chemical batch

    🟢 Fisher D3 — Lined Butterfly

    Wafer-style butterfly valve with PTFE or PFA lining from disc to body — completely isolates the carbon steel body from corrosive process fluids. Quarter-turn for fast on-off or throttling. Pairs with Fisher 1052 or 1061 rotary actuator.

    Cv: Up to 12,000 (14\") | Temp: -40°F to 350°F | Applications: Acid service, caustic, chlorine, fluorinated compounds — any corrosive fluid

    🟢 Fisher ES — Triple-Offset Butterfly

    Metal-seated butterfly valve with triple-offset geometry — zero friction, zero wear, bubble-tight shutoff without seat contact during stroke. Class IV or VI leakage standard. Used wherever globe valves are too expensive and butterfly valves traditionally don't provide tight shutoff.

    Size: 3\" to 48\" | Temp: -320°F to 1400°F | Applications: High-temp steam, cryogenic, refinery CCR, power plant throttling

    🟡 Fisher V-Series Sanitary

    3-A certified sanitary rotary control valve for pharmaceutical, food & beverage, and biotechnology applications. Tri-Clamp connections, electropolished 316L SS, and CIP/SIP capability. Full IQ/OQ documentation package from Applied Control.

    Size: 1\" to 4\" Tri-Clamp | Temp: CIP/SIP to 135°C | Applications: WFI systems, bioreactors, CIP circuits, fill-finish pharmaceutical

    Specialty & High-Performance Valves

    🔴 Fisher MegaStream Choke Valve

    Purpose-built for extreme pressure drop service — wellhead choke, subsea production, HP separator letdown. Hardened trim resists erosion from sand, scale, and produced water. Multi-stage cage trim reduces velocity and extends trim life 3–10× versus standard choke valves.

    ΔP: Up to 10,000 psi single-stage | Applications: Wellhead production choke, gas letdown, HP separator control

    ⚪ Fisher WhisperFlo Noise Trim

    Multi-stage, multi-path noise attenuation cage trim for gas and steam throttling applications exceeding 85 dBA. Reduces aerodynamic noise by 15–30 dB versus standard trim — often eliminates the need for downstream silencers. Applied Control engineers can calculate predicted noise per IEC 60534-8-3 and specify the right trim stage.

    Noise reduction: 15–30 dB | Applications: Gas pressure letdown, compressor recycle, steam conditioning

    ⚪ Fisher Cavitrol III / Anti-Cavitation Trim

    Multi-hole, multi-stage anti-cavitation trim for high-pressure-drop liquid service. Staged pressure recovery prevents vapor bubble collapse inside the valve body — eliminates cavitation damage, noise, and vibration. Applied Control engineers size the Cavitrol trim using Cv and FL calculations per ISA 75.01.

    ΣF correction: Staged to keep P₂ above Pv | Applications: Boiler feedwater, condensate, cooling water letdown, pump recirculation

    🔵 Fisher Cryogenic Valves

    Extended bonnet globe valves for LNG, liquid nitrogen, liquid oxygen, and other cryogenic service. The extended bonnet keeps packing and actuator above the cold zone — prevents ice formation and maintains seal integrity at -320°F. FM Class I Div 1 approved for oxygen service with cleaned-for-oxygen manufacturing.

    Temp: -320°F to ambient | Applications: LNG terminals, air separation, liquid gas storage, semiconductor gas distribution

    Fisher Pressure Regulators

    ⚙️ Fisher 627 Series — General Purpose Regulator

    The most widely used self-operated pressure regulator in industrial applications. Spring-loaded, direct-acting design requires no external power or air supply. Sizes 3/8\" to 2\", cast iron or stainless steel body, setpoints from 1.5\" W.C. to 150 psig. Used for instrument air, plant gas, heating fuel, and utility pressure control.

    Applications: Instrument air, fuel gas, heating systems, loading arms, pilot gas for PRVs

    ⚙️ Fisher 98H Series — High-Pressure Gas Regulator

    For natural gas distribution, meter runs, and industrial gas supply — inlet pressures up to 1,480 psi, outlet setpoints down to 1 psig. Available in pilot-operated configuration for tight output regulation even with large flow swings. Used at wellheads, compressor stations, and plant gate stations.

    Inlet: Up to 1,480 psi | Applications: Gas distribution, meter stations, wellhead regulation, compressor fuel gas

    ⚙️ Fisher 95L Series — Low-Pressure Regulator

    Designed for very low outlet pressures — as low as 0.5\" W.C. (0.02 psi). Precision spring selection gives accurate control at pressures that would be difficult with standard regulators. Common in burner management systems, HVAC gas supply, and laboratory gas distribution.

    Outlet: 0.5\" W.C. to 2 psig | Applications: Burner management, HVAC gas trains, laboratory supply, pilot gas systems

    Fisher Actuators

    The right actuator makes the right valve perform reliably over 20+ years. Applied Control engineers specify and repair the complete Fisher actuator line.

    🔧 Fisher 657 — Diaphragm Actuator (Fail-Open)

    Spring-return diaphragm actuator — air-to-close, fail-open design. The spring extends the actuator stem on air loss, opening the valve. Required wherever process safety demands the valve open on failure: cooling water to reactors, quench systems, emergency dump valves. Sizes 30–100 cm² diaphragm area.

    Fail Position: Open (spring extends) | Air Supply: 20–100 psi | Applications: Cooling water, emergency vent, quench, purge systems

    🔧 Fisher 667 — Diaphragm Actuator (Fail-Closed)

    Spring-return diaphragm actuator — air-to-open, fail-closed design. Spring retracts the stem on air loss, closing the valve. The default actuator choice for feed and product lines, where valve closure on failure is the safe state. Same diaphragm sizes as 657 but reversed spring action.

    Fail Position: Closed (spring retracts) | Air Supply: 20–100 psi | Applications: Feed valves, discharge control, product isolation

    🔧 Fisher 585C / 1305C — Piston Cylinder Actuators

    Double-acting piston actuators for high-output force applications — large valves, high-pressure trim, or fast stroking requirements. Available in spring-return or double-acting configurations. The 1305C handles Fisher HP and large globe valves; the 585C covers general service. Volume boosters add speed for ESD applications.

    Output Force: Up to 30,000 lbs thrust | Applications: Large globes, high-differential-pressure trim, fast-acting ESD service

    🔧 Fisher 1061 — Scotch-Yoke Rotary Actuator

    Pneumatic rotary actuator for quarter-turn valves — ball valves, butterfly valves, Vee-Ball. Scotch-yoke mechanism provides maximum torque at mid-stroke where butterfly valves need it most. Spring-return design for fail-open or fail-closed safety positioning. Integral DVC6200 mount available.

    Output Torque: Up to 180,000 in-lbs | Applications: Fisher D3, ES, Vee-Ball, third-party ball valves, large butterfly valves

    Bettis Actuators — High-Torque & Electric

    ⚡ Bettis G Series — Rack-and-Pinion Rotary

    Compact, lightweight pneumatic quarter-turn actuator for on-off and throttling ball valves. Double-acting or spring-return. Torque range 28–17,700 in-lbs. ATEX and FM approved. Standard ISO 5211 mounting for all major valve brands. Used for automated isolation, on-off control, and modulating service.

    Torque: 28–17,700 in-lbs | Applications: Automated isolation, batch processing, skid packages

    ⚡ Bettis MTQ — High-Torque Scotch-Yoke

    Heavy-duty pneumatic actuator for large-bore ball valves and butterfly valves requiring high torque — pipeline isolation, terminal valves, large refinery gate valves. Modular spring package allows fail-open or fail-closed configuration in the field without replacing the actuator.

    Torque: Up to 2,000,000 in-lbs | Applications: Pipeline isolation, large terminal valves, refinery block valves

    ⚡ Bettis RPC Series — Electric Quarter-Turn

    Electric rotary actuator for locations where instrument air is unavailable or undesirable — remote pipeline valves, unmanned wellpads, HVAC damper control. Battery backup maintains fail-safe positioning on power loss. HART communication for remote diagnostics and position feedback.

    Power: 24V DC / 120V AC / solar-ready | Applications: Remote pipeline, unmanned wellpads, HVAC, water distribution

    Next-generation 80 GHz FMCW radar with 3° beam angle — fits small nozzles, avoids internal obstructions, and provides ±2mm accuracy even on turbulent or foaming surfaces. No moving parts, no process contact. SIL 2. The standard for clean-to-moderately-challenging tank level applications.

    📊 Rosemount 5300 / 5302 / 5303 Guided Wave Radar

    TDR-based guided wave radar for challenging level applications — foam, vapor, turbulence, or interface measurement. The 5302 measures both upper and lower liquid levels simultaneously for oil-water separation. Flexible rope probe for agitated tanks and slurry service. SIL 2 certified.

    Traditional differential pressure level measurement — the lowest-cost option for open vessels and pressurized tanks with known specific gravity. Wet-leg or dry-leg configuration. Flanged or threaded direct-mount to vessel nozzle. When specific gravity is known and constant, DP level is the most reliable technology with no active electronics in the process.

    Principle: P = ρgh | Applications: Open tanks, pressurized vessels, boiler drum level, condensate pots

    📊 Rosemount 2120 Vibrating Fork — Point Level Switch

    Simple, robust point-level switch for high/low alarm — no calibration required. Vibrating tuning fork detects liquid contact and changes output. No process-wetted moving parts. Used as a backup to continuous level transmitters, as an overfill switch, or as a pump dry-run protector.

    Applications: High/low level alarm, pump protection, overfill prevention | SIL Rating: SIL 1/2

    Pressure Relief & Safety Valves

    🔴 Crosby JOS-E / JBS-E Safety Relief Valves

    ASME Section I (steam) and Section VIII (pressure vessel) certified spring-loaded safety relief valves. The JOS-E (jet-type open spring) is the standard for vapor and gas service; the JBS-E adds a balanced bellows for backpressure service up to 50% of set pressure. Applied Control's National Board certified shop tests, repairs, and recertifies Crosby valves per API 576.

    Standards: ASME I, VIII | Testing: National Board certified shop | Learn more: Chapter 7

    🔴 Anderson Greenwood Pilot-Operated PRVs

    Pilot-operated safety relief valves for applications where spring-loaded valves create problems — tight blowdown, high backpressure, cryogenic service, or high-ratio pressure systems. The pilot controls the main valve, allowing operation up to 98% of set pressure (vs. 90% for spring-loaded). Applied Control stocks and services the Type 90, 25 series, and modulating pilot versions.

    Operating Ratio: Up to 98% of set | Applications: High-ratio systems, cryogenic, high backpressure, compressor protection

    🔴 Rupture Discs (Fike / Continental)

    Non-reclosing pressure relief devices — the last line of defense when PRVs are insufficient. Applied Control specifies, sizes, and orders rupture discs for installation upstream of PRVs (to protect valve seats from corrosion) or as standalone protection on reactors and vessels. Burst pressure accuracy ±5% across operating temperature range per ASME code.

    Standards: ASME VIII | Applications: Reactor protection, PRV seat protection, rapid-deflagration relief

    Solenoid Valves — ASCO

    2-way and 3-way solenoid valves for instrument air piloting, process on-off, and SIS shutdown applications. The standard solenoid valve for pneumatic actuator piloting — feeds the DVC6200 or hardwired into the SIS for ESD service.

Applied Control specifies the correct SIL-rated solenoid and provides proof test documentation to satisfy IEC 61511 requirements.

    SIL Rating: SIL 2/3 capable | Standard: IEC 61511 | Applications: ESD systems, SIS shutdown valves, SIL 2/3 final elements
