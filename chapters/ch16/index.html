    Chapter 16: Industry Applications — O&G, Refining, Power, Chemical

    Learning Objectives

    • Remember: Identify the three process units in a refinery that consume the most control valves and name the primary severe-service challenge in each
    • Understand: Explain why crude distillation unit (CDU) overhead control valves require special trim and what happens without it
    • Apply: Specify the correct Fisher product family, trim type, and actuator for a refinery coker main fractionator bottoms control valve service
    • Analyze: Compare the instrumentation requirements of a natural gas processing plant to a petrochemical complex, focusing on measurement accuracy, flow custody, and safety system integration
    • Evaluate: Identify the three highest-value Applied Control opportunities in a coal-fired power plant and prioritize the sales conversation

    16.1 Oil & Gas Production

    Upstream Oil & Gas — Wellhead and Gathering

    Upstream production begins at the wellhead where produced fluids (oil, gas, water, sand) flow under reservoir pressure. Control valves in upstream service face the harshest conditions in any industry.

    Wellhead choke valves: - Pressure: 3,000–15,000 PSIG wellhead shut-in pressure - Fluid: Mixed phase — gas, oil, and produced water with sand and solids - Challenge: Sand erosion destroys standard trim; high ΔP causes severe cavitation in liquid-heavy streams and high-velocity noise in gas-heavy streams - Solution: Fisher severe service choke valves with tungsten carbide or ceramic trim; staged pressure reduction for liquid service (Cavitrol); bean-type orifice for gas wells

    Production separator control: - Level control on three-phase separators (oil/water/gas) - Fisher V150 Vee-Ball for oil and water leg control (handles produced water solids) - Fisher EZ or ED for gas back-pressure control - Rosemount 5302 GWR for oil/water interface level

    Flow measurement: - Rosemount 3051 with orifice plate or Annubar: gas and liquid well test measurement - Micro Motion Coriolis: custody transfer measurement at lease automatic custody transfer (LACT) units - Rosemount 8800D: flare gas measurement for environmental reporting

    Midstream — Gas Processing

    Natural gas processing plants remove liquids (NGLs — ethane, propane, butane) and impurities (CO₂, H₂S) from the gas stream before pipeline transmission.

    Key applications: - Amine absorber/regenerator (acid gas removal): Control valves handling hot amine (H₂S-containing); pitting corrosion from carbonic acid; Fisher D3 or ED with Hastelloy trim - Refrigeration/turboexpander: Anti-surge valve on compressor recycle; extremely fast-stroking (volume booster + DVC6200); Fisher ED with volume booster - NGL fractionation: Depropanizer, deethanizer, debutanizer column control; Fisher HP or ED; column differential pressure for tray efficiency

    Measurement: - Chromatography (GC): Composition measurement for BTU content and custody transfer quality - Coriolis: Liquid NGL custody transfer at truck/rail loading racks - Vortex (8800D): Plant fuel gas metering

    16.2 Petroleum Refining

    A refinery is the highest-density concentration of critical control valve service in any industry. A mid-size 200,000 BPD refinery may contain 2,000–5,000 control valves; a significant percentage are severe service.

    Crude Distillation Unit (CDU)

    The CDU separates crude oil into fractions by boiling point. It is the most important unit in the refinery — a CDU shutdown stops the entire plant.

    Key CDU control valve applications:

    Application Service challenge Fisher solution
    Atmospheric column overhead Corrosive HCl/H₂O condensate; erosion from naphthas Fisher HP with Hastelloy C wetted parts; PTFE packing
    Atmospheric column bottoms High temperature (650°F+); high viscosity; coking Fisher HP-20 high-temp design; insulating extension bonnet
    Crude charge heater fuel gas Combustion safety (fail-closed); fast response Fisher EZ with 667 (fail-closed) and DVC6200 PST
    Atmospheric column side streams Moderate service; high cycle frequency Fisher ED with DVC6200
    Vacuum column overhead Very low pressure (vacuum); large flow capacity; condensables Fisher ET or HP with large Cv; special vacuum trim

    Fluid Catalytic Cracking (FCC)

    FCC converts heavy gas oils into more valuable gasoline and diesel using a fluidized catalyst system at 900–1100°F.

    Severe service challenges: - Catalyst carryover in gas streams creates severe erosion - High temperature requires exotic metallurgy - Flue gas from catalyst regenerator: high-temperature, corrosive, particulate-laden

    Fisher solutions for FCC: - Slide gate valves (Fisher proprietary) for catalyst circulation — standard globe valves cannot handle catalyst-laden flow - High-temperature HP valves with Stellite-faced trim for erosive service - WhisperFlo cage trim for flue gas noise control

    Hydroprocessing (Hydrotreater, Hydrocracker)

    Hydrotreating removes sulfur, nitrogen, and metals from distillate streams using high-pressure hydrogen (500–2500 PSIG) at elevated temperature (500–800°F).

    Critical considerations: - Hydrogen embrittlement: Carbon steel fails in hydrogen service above 300°F — Nelson curves (API 941) define the allowable temperature/pressure combinations for each steel alloy. Chrome-moly (1.25 Cr, 2.25 Cr) is required for high-temperature hydrogen service. - HTHA (High Temperature Hydrogen Attack): Hydrogen reacts with carbides in carbon steel at elevated temperature, weakening the steel. Fisher HP and specialty valves use appropriate Cr-Mo alloys for hydroprocessing service. - High pressure: Class 1500–2500 valves; Fisher HP series covers these classes

    Power Generation — Thermal Power Plants

    Coal-fired and gas-fired power plants are among Applied Control's most important customers because of the critical nature of the control functions involved.

    Steam turbine control: - HP and IP turbine admission/control valves: High-pressure, high-temperature steam (1050°F, 2400 PSIG in supercritical plants); Class 2500 Fisher EHD valves - Turbine bypass: Routes steam directly to condenser during load rejection; must stroke fully in < 2 seconds; Fisher HP with volume booster - Reheat stop valve: Safety isolation on reheated steam; Fisher HP with 667 fail-closed

    Feedwater control: - Boiler feedwater control valve: The most critical valve in the plant — controls boiler drum level and feedwater flow at very high ΔP (900 PSIG typical); Class 600–1500 Fisher HP - Startup feedwater bypass: Small valve for low-flow startup control; Fisher ED

    Combustion air and fuel gas: - Fuel gas control: Fail-closed (667); DVC6200 with partial stroke testing for SIL compliance - Combustion air dampers: Large-area modulating air control; butterfly valves with electric actuators

    Emissions control: - SCR (Selective Catalytic Reduction) ammonia injection: Precise NH₃ dosing for NOₓ control; Fisher ED or EZ with Rosemount flow measurement - FGD (Flue Gas Desulfurization) slurry control: Abrasive limestone slurry; Fisher Vee-Ball or eccentric plug valves

    Key Applied Control message for power: "Every MW of generation lost to a valve-caused trip costs dispatch revenue. Our DVC6200 diagnostic program prevents the valve trips that cost you generation."

    16.3 Chemical and Petrochemical

    Ethylene Cracking Furnace

    Ethylene is produced by thermal cracking of naphtha or ethane in radiant furnace tubes at 1400–1600°F. The furnace outlet quench system requires very fast-acting, high-temperature valves.

    Transfer line exchangers (TLE) isolation: Must close within 0.5 seconds on furnace trip; Fisher Y-design 3-way valves; volume boosters for fast stroke; tight shutoff required.

    Furnace feed control: - Naphtha/steam feed: Flow ratio control; Fisher ED with DVC6200 - Steam dilution: Precise steam-to-hydrocarbon ratio; Fisher EZ or ED

    Distillation Column Control

    Distillation is the workhorse separation process in petrochemicals. A typical large distillation column has:

    • Overhead condenser control: Reflux flow, overhead product, vent; Fisher ED
    • Reboiler duty control: Steam to reboiler; Fisher EZ or ED (fail-closed on steam)
    • Feed preheat: Feed temperature; Fisher EZ
    • Bottoms level: Column sump; Fisher Vee-Ball for high-viscosity or solids-containing bottoms
    • Column differential pressure: Rosemount 3051 ΔP for tray efficiency monitoring

    Distillation cascade control in DeltaV: Composition control (analyzer feedback) → inferential temperature control → flow control cascade — a classic three-level cascade that DeltaV implements with HART integration from bottom to top.

    Reactor Temperature Control

    Exothermic chemical reactors require precise temperature control — too cold = poor conversion; too hot = runaway reaction.

    Cooling water jacket control: - Typical: One control valve on cooling water inlet (temperature feedback from reactor) - Advanced: Cascade — outer loop reactor temperature, inner loop coolant outlet temperature - Safety: High temperature → SIS activates emergency quench; DeltaV SIS with DVC6200 PST

    Critical sizing: The cooling control valve must have sufficient Cv to handle emergency cooling loads — not just normal operating flow. Oversizing the valve too much creates throttling control issues; undersizing creates inability to cool during exotherms.

    Chapter Summary

    Oil and gas upstream service is the most severe in the industry — wellhead choke valves with sand erosion, produced water with solids, and extreme wellhead pressures demand Fisher severe service products and tungsten carbide or ceramic trim. Refining concentrates critical service in CDU, FCC, and hydroprocessing units — requiring high-temperature, corrosion-resistant metallurgy, noise-attenuating trim, and explosive-area-certified positioners. Power generation centers on steam turbine admission and feedwater control — fast-stroking, high-pressure, SIL-certified valves. Chemical and petrochemical plants combine distillation control (precise, high-cycle), reactor safety (SIS integration, PST), and specialized severe service (cracking furnace, slurry). In every industry, Applied Control's message is the same: engineering depth (Fisher specification and sizing), diagnostic capability (DVC6200+AMS), and repair expertise (National Board valve shop) create value no catalog distributor can match.
